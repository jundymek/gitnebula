## Story

As a reader whose `analysis.json` is missing, malformed or from another
version,
I want the viewer to tell me what went wrong and what to do about it,
So that a failed load is a message rather than a blank page.

## Context (binding)

- `epics.md` Epic 6; AD-7 (error policy: abort with the fixed shape
  `«stage»: «cause» — «remedy»`), AD-12 (the data-loading contract),
  NFR-11 (no contract change).
- **Why the first half exists.** `renderErrorScreen`
  (`packages/viz/src/error-screen.ts:11`) has **no test in any harness** —
  not in vitest, not in Playwright — while being the first and only thing a
  reader sees when a load fails. It is called from three places in `app.ts`
  (lines 123, 340, 371), and `loader.ts` constructs failures in six places
  across three kinds: `unreachable`, `malformed` (four constructions) and
  `unsupported-version`.
- **The `file://` case is the one a human actually hits**, by double-clicking
  the bundle instead of serving it; `loader.ts:62-64` answers it with
  `FILE_PROTOCOL_HINT`.
- **The screen is built with `textContent`, not `innerHTML`, deliberately** —
  `error-screen.ts:4` says why: the strings it shows come from the document it
  failed to trust. A test must prove that property still holds.
- **Why the second half exists — the seam.** Two validators guard the same
  document and **nothing checks them against each other**:
  - `packages/cli/src/assemble.ts` validates with **ajv** against
    `packages/contract/src/analysis.schema.json`, which is
    `additionalProperties: false` on the document root and on every definition
    (`node`, `edge`, `cochange`, `repo`).
  - `packages/viz/src/loader.ts` re-checks with **hand-written predicates over
    a subset of fields** — deliberately, to keep the bundle self-contained and
    free of a validator dependency (AD-8, AD-12).

  Measured on 2026-09-05, and these are the numbers your story is built on:
  - the schema's `node.required` has **12** entries — `id`, `kind`, `parent`,
    `path`, `layer`, `loc`, `churn`, `commits`, `authors`, `lastChangedAt`,
    `description`, `descriptionSource` — while `isNodeShaped`
    (`loader.ts:164-175`) checks **6**: `id`, `kind`, `path`, `layer`, `loc`,
    `churn`. Unchecked: `parent`, `commits`, `authors`, `lastChangedAt`,
    `description`, `descriptionSource`.
  - the schema's `edge.required` has **4** — `source`, `target`, `kind`,
    `weight` — while `isEdgeShaped` (`loader.ts:177-183`) checks **2**.
    Unchecked: `kind`, `weight`.
  - `cochanges` is in the document root's `required`, and the loader asserts
    only that it **is an array** (`loader.ts:195-196`), never inspecting an
    element — while the blast-radius panel reads `cochanges[].{a,b,count}`.
- **This is the shape of defect that survives per-branch review**: each side is
  correct on its own terms, and the gap only exists between them. That is why
  it is in this epic.
- **Report, do not widen the loader.** Loosening or tightening `loader.ts`'s
  predicates is a contract-adjacent decision: `CLAUDE.md` says changing the
  contract "requires its own story, a schema version decision, and an ADR if
  the change is structural", and the loader's narrowness is a deliberate
  design choice, not an oversight. **Your job is to measure the consequence and
  write it down**, not to close the gap. If the measurement argues for closing
  it, say so in the README and let the maintainer schedule it.
- **What "measure the consequence" means concretely.** For each unchecked
  field, determine in the browser whether the viewer **degrades safely** (the
  reader sees an honest absence) or **misreads it** (the reader sees a wrong
  number, or the page breaks). Those are different findings and the story must
  distinguish them. A missing `commits` that renders as "no data" is fine; a
  missing `count` on a co-change pair that renders as `undefined commits` is
  not.
- **What belongs in a browser here, and what does not.** `loader.ts`'s
  predicates and `checkVersion` are pure functions over parsed JSON: testing
  *them* belongs in vitest, and `packages/viz/src/loader.test.ts` already
  exists. What is browser-only is the **assembled path** — a real fetch of
  `analysis.json` over HTTP, `boot()` choosing the error screen over an engine,
  and `renderErrorScreen` producing something a reader can act on. If an
  assertion you are writing would pass in jsdom, it belongs in vitest and not
  in this suite. Check `loader.test.ts` before writing each case, and say in
  the README what you found already covered.
- **Fixtures.** Start from a valid document — `root-files` is the smallest one
  carrying a co-change pair — and mutate copies for the failure cases. Do
  **not** add a fixture to `packages/contract/fixtures/`: those are the
  contract's committed examples and every one of them is valid by design. Your
  malformed documents are test material and live with your specs.
- **`GITNEBULA_FIXTURE` 404s loudly on a missing name, on purpose** — story 1.4
  learned that a silent fallback produces a run that looks normal while
  measuring nothing. That behaviour is itself worth an assertion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Each of the loader's three failure kinds — `unreachable`, `malformed`,
  `unsupported-version` — renders an error screen in the browser carrying its
  title and its detail, asserted by text. Before this story `renderErrorScreen`
  had no test in any harness, and the README says so.
- AC-2: The `file://` case shows the protocol hint, because that is the failure
  a reader reaches by double-clicking the bundle rather than serving it.
- AC-3: A failure detail containing markup is displayed as text and creates no
  element — the `textContent` property `error-screen.ts:4` documents is proven,
  not assumed.
- AC-4: The story records the measured seam between the two validators: 12
  required node fields against 6 checked, 4 required edge fields against 2
  checked, and `cochanges` asserted only to be an array. The numbers are
  re-derived by the agent from the schema and the loader rather than copied
  from this spec, and any discrepancy with the figures above is reported.
- AC-5: A document that ajv would reject **loads and renders** in the browser,
  demonstrating the seam rather than describing it; and for each unchecked
  field the story states whether the viewer degrades safely or misreads it,
  distinguishing the two.
- AC-6: The gap is **reported, not patched**. `packages/viz/src/loader.ts` and
  `packages/cli/src/assemble.ts` are unmodified, and the README states what a
  future story would have to decide — including whether the loader should
  widen at all, given that its narrowness is deliberate.
- AC-7: `analysis.schema.json` is byte-unchanged, `schemaVersion` is still
  `"1.0"` (NFR-11), no fixture is added to `packages/contract/fixtures/`, and
  every new spec is a **new file** under `packages/viz/ui/tests/`.
- AC-8 (human-review): The three error screens read as helpful to someone who
  has not read the code — each names a cause and an exit. Recorded as an
  unticked item in `MANUAL_TESTING.md`.
