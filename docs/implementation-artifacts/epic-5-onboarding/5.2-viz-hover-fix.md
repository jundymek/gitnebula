## Story

As a developer moving the pointer across a dense map,
I want hover to reveal a chain without extinguishing everything else,
So that the map stops strobing under the cursor.

## Context (binding)

- PRD FR-29 (supersedes FR-17's *mechanism*, not its intent); NFR-7, UX-DR11
  (reduced motion), UX-DR5 (chain alphas), NFR-11 (no contract change).
- **Why this story exists.** Measured on a real langgraph checkout: a hovered
  node dims **647 of 650 nodes** to `NODE_ALPHA_DIMMED`, because the median
  1-hop chain is **3 nodes**. Pointer hit-areas cover **78% of the viewport
  at 6× zoom**, so the cursor is almost always over something and the map
  strobes as it moves. Dimming-as-mechanism does not survive this density.
- **Current constants** (`engine/constants.ts`): `NODE_ALPHA_DIMMED = 0.1`,
  `EDGE_ALPHA_DIMMED = 0.03`, `EDGE_ALPHA_CHAIN = 0.62`,
  `EDGE_ALPHA_BASE = 0.2`.
- **Decision taken by the maintainer, binding on this story.** The resting
  opacity for out-of-chain elements is **0.55 for nodes and 0.12 for edges**.
  The chain is carried by emphasis — brightness, a ring, `EDGE_ALPHA_CHAIN`
  — not by everything else disappearing. Do not re-derive these values;
  record any measured reason they cannot hold in `DECISIONS.md` and report it
  rather than silently changing them.
- **A prototype exists** for the density problem this story shares with 5.4:
  `git stash show -p stash@{1}` — a label budget plus a first-come-wins
  occupancy grid in `render.ts`, with its measurements in the comments (405
  file labels arrive at once at `FILE_LABEL_ZOOM`, 485 at 6×). It is
  experimental and uncommitted; read it as evidence and prior art, adopt only
  what this story's ACs need, and do not treat it as a base to apply.
- Story 3.4's isolate action and story 3.3's search-arrival pulse both ride
  the same render state. This story changes the hover *encoding* only.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: On hover, out-of-chain nodes render at **0.55** opacity and
  out-of-chain edges at **0.12**, replacing the previous
  `NODE_ALPHA_DIMMED`/`EDGE_ALPHA_DIMMED` values on the hover path; the
  hovered node and its one-hop chain are marked by emphasis (brightness, a
  ring, and `EDGE_ALPHA_CHAIN` on chain edges) and remain unambiguously
  identifiable. Asserted by encoding tests over the render state, not by
  screenshot (FR-29, FR-17's intent).
- AC-2: Hover exit restores full opacity, and hover remains suppressed during
  a pan (existing behaviour from story 3.3 — a regression test asserts it
  still holds).
- AC-3: A pointer crossing many nodes in quick succession introduces no
  frame-level flicker: the transition between hover states is stable, with no
  frame in which the previous chain has been cleared and the new one not yet
  applied. Asserted on the deterministic frame clock.
- AC-4: The isolate action (story 3.4) and the search-arrival pulse (story
  3.3) keep working unchanged — this story changes neither selection nor
  isolate semantics, and their existing tests pass without modification.
- AC-5: Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, no animated transition is introduced
  on hover state change (NFR-7, UX-DR11).
- AC-6 (human-review): Sweeping the pointer across a real 650-node map no
  longer reads as strobing, and the chain is still findable at a glance.
  Recorded as an unticked item in `MANUAL_TESTING.md`.
